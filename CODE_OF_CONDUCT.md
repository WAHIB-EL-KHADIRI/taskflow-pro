# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes contributions from anyone. Participants are expected to be
respectful and constructive.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- harassment of any kind, public or private
- publishing others' private information without permission
- sustained disruption of discussions or reviews

Criticism of code is welcome and expected. Criticism of people is not.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to
public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **wahibelkhadiri06@gmail.com**. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or
block accounts as needed.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
